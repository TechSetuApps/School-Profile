# 📌 +2 High School Pindaruch

> **Empowering Students Through Skill-First Education & Aptitude-Guided Pathways**

Welcome to the official documentation and overview repository for **+2 High School Pindaruch**, a higher secondary educational institution located in Pindaruch village, Keoti block, Darbhanga, Bihar. Our school caters to students from **Class 6 to Class 12**, blending rigorous academic learning with athletic excellence and personalized guidance.
