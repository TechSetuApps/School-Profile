About the school

A higher secondary school built around what each child is good at.

Serving Pindaruch and the surrounding villages of Darbhanga district, with classes from 6 to 12 and four guided pathways after Class 10.

+2 High School Pindaruch takes students through Classes 6 to 12, with the higher secondary (+2) wing offering a choice of academic streams alongside a structured sports programme. The school's approach is simple: identify what a student is naturally strong at, and build their remaining school years around it.

Rather than pushing every student down the same road, teachers assess ability early — in the classroom, on the field, and through aptitude — and use that to guide each student toward the stream or path where they are most likely to succeed. A child strong in numbers moves toward Commerce; one strong in expression moves toward Arts; one strong on the field gets a real sports track, not just an occasional games period.

The campus sits at the heart of Pindaruch village, surrounded by open ground used for assembly, sport and outdoor learning. The atmosphere is calm, ordered and serious about learning — but never rigid.

How students are guided

After Class 10, every student is placed on the path that fits them.

The school's core philosophy — every child moves forward on the path suited to their skill, not the path the crowd is on.

After Class 10, every student goes through an internal assessment covering academic performance, physical aptitude and classroom observation. Based on this, class teachers and the guidance panel recommend a stream — the student and family make the final choice. No student is left to figure it out alone.

Sports at Pindaruch

Treated as a genuine academic track, not an extracurricular add-on.

Sport is one of the four guided pathways — selected the same way academic streams are.

Sport is one of the four guided pathways at the school. Students who show strong physical or competitive ability are trained daily alongside their academics, not instead of them.

Selection happens the same way as academic streaming — through observation, practice performance and PE faculty recommendation — so no talented student is overlooked simply because they're stronger on the field than in the classroom.

The open ground at the centre of campus hosts morning assembly, athletics and inter-house sport, with coaching built into the daily timetable rather than squeezed in at the end of the day.
